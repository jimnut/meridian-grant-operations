Questions, answered plainly

Is GrantConsole the right kind of grant software?

The public demo contains two organizations, eighteen grants and realistic sample workflows, so you can evaluate the product before a conversation.

What is GrantConsole?

GrantConsole is post-award grant management software for nonprofit grant recipients. It helps teams track deadlines, restricted budgets, evidence, risk and funder reports for grants they have already been awarded.

Is GrantConsole a fundraising CRM or a grant-discovery database?

No. GrantConsole does not manage donors and does not find grant opportunities. It starts where fundraising ends: after the award.

How does GrantConsole handle restricted grant budgets?

Budget burn is tracked against the grant period, so teams can see whether a restricted budget is spending too quickly or too slowly and act before the period ends.

What are GrantConsole's 11 risk rules?

GrantConsole evaluates active grants against 11 explainable risk rules. When a rule flags a grant, the reason and supporting records are shown so staff can verify the warning.

Can I try GrantConsole without a sales call?

Yes. A live demo seeded with example nonprofit data is publicly available without a sales call.
