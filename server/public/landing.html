Questions grant teams ask

What is post-award grant management?

Everything that happens after a grant is won: meeting reporting deadlines, spending restricted funds as budgeted, collecting the evidence funders require, completing compliance tasks and preparing renewals. GrantConsole focuses entirely on this stage.

How is this different from a fundraising CRM?

A CRM manages donors and asks. GrantConsole manages obligations — the deadlines, restricted budgets and reports attached to money you already hold. Many teams run both, side by side.

Can it track restricted funds?

Yes — per grant, to the cent, against the elapsed grant period. A grant 40% through its period with 70% of its budget spent is flagged as ahead of schedule, with the numbers shown.

What does the reporting packet include?

Budget against actuals, deliverables with attached evidence, open risks and the complete activity trail — assembled per grant, designed for print/save-as-PDF, with CSV exports alongside.

Who is it for?

Small and midsize nonprofits juggling several restricted grants at once — grants managers, finance leads and executive directors sharing one workspace with role-based access.
